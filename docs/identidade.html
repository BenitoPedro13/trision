The thesis

A frame is a decision
about what you look at.

The mark is a frame around a letter. The product is a frame around an eye. And the business is a frame around a catalogue — each reseller shows a cut of Amanda's stock. The same objects, framed a different way.

01 The find

This was not brought to Trísion.
It was already in the logo.

The four corners around the Tr are a visor — the same device as a crop mark, a camera's focus reticle, and a pair of frames. It has never been used for anything. It becomes the system's only ornament.

the mark

a frame around a letter

Four corners locked into the Tr, the T stem dropping through the brackets.

the product

a frame around an eye

It is literally what she sells. The object and the mark have the same shape.

the business

a frame around a catalogue

Each reseller storefront is a cut of the same stock. The data model is the brand.

04 Numeração · the technical voice

The number is already stamped
inside the temple.

5218-145

The boxing system marking is stamped on every pair of frames in the world. It is the equivalent of F&A Móveis' 2,30 m: an industry artifact that customers half-recognise and that no competitor treats as typography.

52 mm
lens width
□ 18 mm
bridge — U+25A1, drawn as SVG because almost no mono ships the glyph
145 mm
temple

Stored as three numbers in mm, formatted at the edge. Never "52□18-145" as a string — a string cannot be filtered, sorted or converted. A product with no measurement renders no numeração; it does not render a placeholder.

05 Type

Squareness lives in the structure,
not in the typeface.

Setting the whole site in a techno face like Eurostile would be loud, would age badly and would compete with the logo. Variable Archivo covers everything with one width axis: headlines at wdth 118 echo the logotype's extension, body at wdth 100 stays readable. One font file.

The logotype is never set in a typeface. It ships as SVG. If the original vector does not turn up, it is redrawn by hand from the raster — a near-miss font is worse than no wordmark.

06 The platform · what the design does not show

One catalogue.
Many storefronts.

The asymmetry is the product: the catalogue belongs to the brand and is not tenant-scoped; what each reseller owns is a selection. That is why produtos stays out of the multi-tenant plugin and mostruario stays in.
