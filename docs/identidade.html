The thesis

A frame is a decision
about what you look at.

The mark is a frame around a letter. The product is a frame around an eye. And the business is a frame around a catalogue — each reseller shows a cut of Amanda's stock. The same objects, framed a different way.

01 The find

This was not brought to Trísion.
It was already in the logo.

The four corners around the Tr are a visor — the same device as a crop mark, a camera's focus reticle, and a pair of frames. It has never been used for anything. It becomes the system's only ornament.

the mark

a frame around a letter

Four corners locked into the Tr, the T stem dropping through the brackets.

the product

a frame around an eye

It is literally what she sells. The object and the mark have the same shape.

the business

a frame around a catalogue

Each reseller storefront is a cut of the same stock. The data model is the brand.

02 Colour · values measured, not chosen by eye

Her gold was never unreadable.
White was.

Amanda stated the brand colour is gold with black. That outranks the turquoise we inferred from her portrait (spec-brand.md §1.5b). The gold is sampled from her own lockup — not picked from the stock-gold shelf.

#CCA866

She moved Instagram to black-and-white because it is easier to read. She was right, and it is measurable:

  • Gold on white: 2.24 — fails WCAG AA.
  • Gold on --noite: 8.42 — passes comfortably.

The problem was never gold as identity. It was gold as body ink on a light field. The ground this site already had is the only one her real brand colour has ever worked on.

04 Numeração · the technical voice

The number is already stamped
inside the temple.

5218-145

The boxing system marking is stamped on every pair of frames in the world. It is the equivalent of F&A Móveis' 2,30 m: an industry artifact that customers half-recognise and that no competitor treats as typography.

52 mm
lens width
□ 18 mm
bridge — U+25A1, drawn as SVG because almost no mono ships the glyph
145 mm
temple

Stored as three numbers in mm, formatted at the edge. Never "52□18-145" as a string — a string cannot be filtered, sorted or converted. A product with no measurement renders no numeração; it does not render a placeholder.

05 Type

Squareness lives in the structure,
not in the typeface.

Setting the whole site in a techno face like Eurostile would be loud, would age badly and would compete with the logo. Variable Archivo covers everything with one width axis: headlines at wdth 118 echo the logotype's extension, body at wdth 100 stays readable. One font file. Technical layer is IBM Plex Mono.

06 The platform · what the design does not show

One catalogue.
Many storefronts.

The asymmetry is the product: the catalogue belongs to the brand and is not tenant-scoped; what each reseller owns is a selection. That is why produtos stays out of the multi-tenant plugin and mostruario stays in.

07 What blocks

Three questions hold code.

Does Amanda own a domain? Wildcard subdomains require an apex. Today she is on sitetrision.my.canva.site, which suggests she does not. That blocks all of Fase 1 and is the first purchase of the project.

spec-brand.md §6 #4

Is price one suggested retail, or does each reseller set their own? Decides whether mostruario.preco exists — and whether "register the product once" survives contact with reality. Do not build a per-reseller price field speculatively.

spec-brand.md §6 #7

Does the WhatsApp button reach Amanda or the reseller? destinoLead exists so this does not block the build, but it must be answered before launch — it changes what the reseller thinks they bought.
